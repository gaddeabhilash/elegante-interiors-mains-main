# Orniva Materials Guide

## HDHMR

High Density High Moisture Resistant board is durable, smooth, and suitable for wardrobes, TV units, and modular kitchens.

Advantages:

* Moisture resistant
* Smooth finish
* Cost effective

## Plywood

Plywood is one of the most durable materials used in interior furniture.

Advantages:

* Strong and long-lasting
* Better screw holding
* Suitable for kitchens and wardrobes

## BWP Plywood

Boiling Water Proof plywood offers superior moisture resistance.

Best For:

* Kitchens
* Utility areas
* Moisture-prone environments

## Laminates

Affordable and versatile surface finish available in many colors and textures.

## Acrylic Finish

Provides a premium glossy appearance and modern aesthetic.

## Veneer

Natural wood finish that delivers warmth and elegance.

## Quartz

Engineered stone commonly used for kitchen countertops due to its durability and low maintenance.

Material recommendations depend on budget, usage, durability requirements, and aesthetic preferences.
